### Summary of Supplied Sources

#### Source 1: Quarterly Report (Q1) - Aurora Systems Ltd.
- **Revenue and Growth**: The company reported a revenue of 2.4 million EUR, marking an 18% increase year over year. The subscription tier contributed significantly, accounting for 61% of total revenue.
- **Financial Metrics**: The gross margin remained stable at 72%, while operating expenses increased by 11%, which is below the anticipated ceiling of 14%.
- **Customer Metrics**: Aurora Systems gained 312 new customers, with a churn rate reduced to 2.1% from 2.8%. The cash position is reported at 5.9 million EUR.
- **Regional Performance**: Growth was noted across regions: EMEA (22%), Americas (15%), and APAC (31%). APAC is projected to surpass EMEA as the largest market within two years.
- **Product Developments**: The analytics dashboard has exited beta with 4,100 weekly active users, and a mobile app rebuild is scheduled for Q3.
- **Risks**: The company faces risks from reliance on a single hosting provider for 80% of its workloads and limited currency hedging in APAC.
- **Outlook**: For Q2, management anticipates revenue between 2.5 and 2.6 million EUR and plans to continue hiring in engineering.

#### Source 2: Project Phoenix - Weekly Meeting Notes
- **Migration Status**: The migration to PostgreSQL 16 is 80% complete, with a target cutover date of April 3, although this may be delayed.
- **Performance Improvements**: Median API latency improved from 340 ms to 210 ms due to caching, but the p99 latency remains at 1.8 seconds.
- **Budget**: Cloud spending for February was 41,200 EUR, which is 9% under the forecast. An additional cost of approximately 600 EUR per month is expected from a new observability stack starting in April.
- **Action Items**: Specific tasks were assigned to team members, including drafting a rollback plan and obtaining GDPR sign-off for analytics export.
- **Next Meeting**: Scheduled for March 19 at 10:00 in Room 2B. 

This summary encapsulates the key points from both sources, providing insights into the financial performance of Aurora Systems Ltd. and the ongoing developments in Project Phoenix.
